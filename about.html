ABOUT CHALLENGEHUB

Built for learners.
Designed to challenge knowledge.

ChallengeHub is an educational practice platform where secondary-school learners prepare with O'Level past questions and higher-institution students challenge their knowledge through question-based practice.

Why we built ChallengeHub

Preparing for examinations takes more than reading notes. Learners need opportunities to practise, test their understanding, identify gaps, and improve. ChallengeHub brings question-based learning and academic challenges into one platform.

Our focus is to support O'Level candidates with past-question practice and give students in higher institutions a way to challenge and assess their subject knowledge.
